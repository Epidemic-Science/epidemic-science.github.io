Epidemic Science Hub

Three research laboratories working together to understand how infectious diseases spread and how to stop them

Mission

The Epidemic Science Hub brings together researchers at the Indiana University School of Public Health - Bloomington to advance the science of infectious disease epidemiology and translate it into actionable insights for public health

What we do

  • Develop mathematical, computational, and statistical models of infectious disease transmission
  • Study the human, social, and ecological drivers of epidemics, including vectors such as mosquitoes
  • Forecast disease activity and evaluate interventions to support public health decision-making
  • Train the next generation of epidemic scientists and public health professionals
