A new paper in *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* introduces VIBES, a multiscale
modeling approach that integrates within-host and between-hosts dynamics in epidemics.

The work was led by Paulo C. Ventura with colleagues from the CEPH Lab and SEAL Lab and
collaborators in Japan, Italy and the United States.
