A preprint evaluating the trend in Flu forecasts submitted to CDC's FluSight challenge, which uses flu 
forecasting to anticipate changes in influenza activity and inform what may happen in the weeks ahead. Forecasts 
complement influenza surveillance and can support public health planning and communication.

Most models outperformed a simple baseline, and the FluSight ensemble ranked near the top in two
recent seasons, although accuracy dropped when trends were changing quickly. Contributors include
members of the CEPH Lab and SEALab.
