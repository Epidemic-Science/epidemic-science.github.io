The M.S. in Biostatistics at the Indiana University School of Public Health - Bloomington now
offers an **Infectious Disease Modeling (IDM) track**. It gives specialized training in applying
statistical, mathematical and computational methods to public health problems, and it is the
track closest to the Hub's work on epidemic modeling and forecasting.

The 36-credit track pairs core biostatistics with courses in applied geospatial analytics,
population-level analytics and computational epidemiology, plus infectious disease epidemiology,
two biostatistics electives and a research thesis. Students can carry out their thesis research
in one of the Hub's labs.

The full course list and credit breakdown are on our
[M.S. in Biostatistics page]({{ '/programs/ms-biostatistics/' | relative_url }}), and the official
requirements are in the
[IU Bulletin {{ site.data.ms_biostatistics.bulletin_year }}]({{ site.data.ms_biostatistics.bulletin_url }}).
