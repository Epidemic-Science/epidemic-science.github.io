Getting to the Hub

Finding Our Building
Please note that while we are part of the School of Public Health (main building located at 1025 E 7th St), the Hub's labs are located in the SPH Annex. The Annex is situated just across the street at 309 N Woodlawn Ave, near the intersection of 7th Street and Woodlawn Avenue.

Public Transit & Biking

  • Bus: The Annex is easily accessible via Bloomington Transit and the IU Campus Bus system. Several routes drop off near the Indiana Memorial Union (IMU). You can check the current Bloomington Transit schedules or the IU Mobile app for routes serving the 7th & Woodlawn area.
  • Bicycle: If you are cycling to campus, there are bike racks conveniently located directly behind the Annex.

Parking Information

  • Visitor Parking: The most convenient visitor parking is available at the Indiana Memorial Union (IMU) Pay Lot, located just a short walk south of the Annex on Woodlawn Avenue.
  • Campus Permits: If you hold an IU parking permit, please consult the interactive IU Campus Map for the nearest applicable EM or CH permit lots.
  • Street Parking: Please note that metered street parking is generally located off-campus closer to downtown Bloomington, which will require a longer walk to the Annex.
