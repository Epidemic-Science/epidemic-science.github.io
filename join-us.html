Degree Programs

Graduate Programs

Students can join the Hub's labs through degree programs offered by the Indiana University School of Public Health - Bloomington, including:

  • PhD in Epidemiology
  • PhD in Biostatistics
  • MS in Biostatistics

Admissions are handled centrally by the university. Please review the official degree requirements and deadlines before applying.

Undergraduate Research

IU undergraduates seeking research experience are encouraged to reach out to any of the Hub's labs. Students assist with data analysis, literature reviews, and modeling projects that build quantitative skills for careers in academia and public health.
