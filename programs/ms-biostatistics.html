Studying with the Hub

M.S. students can carry out their thesis research in one of the Hub's labs. The Infectious Disease Modeling track is the closest match to our work: it pairs core biostatistics with geospatial analytics, population-level analytics and computational epidemiology. To talk about a thesis project, see Join Us.
